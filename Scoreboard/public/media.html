Replay saves the last seconds of the stream from OBS's replay buffer into … and plays the clip straight away on the replay scene; the dropdown keeps the last 10 clips (newest first) so the best points can be shown again during a pause with Play. The scorebug is hidden for the whole replay (the REPLAY cover takes its place) and comes back, if it was showing, when OBS switches back to the live scene at the end of the clip. The clip length, the folder and the scene names are set on the Admin tab, card Commercial break settings.

Commercials

Play all commercials (and the automatic break after a match) runs the spots listed in obs-settings.json one after another — no merged file needed. The dropdown holds every video file in the commercials folder …; Play puts just that file on the stream. Either way: the scorebug hides, OBS switches to the commercials scene, the file is loaded into the media source, and OBS returns to the live scene when it ends. After a single video the score always comes back (they are meant for during the game). After Play all or the automatic break, the score comes back only if it interrupted a live match; after a finished match it stays hidden until the next match starts.
