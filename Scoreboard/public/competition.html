Competition

Creates every tie of Etapa I (Brașov): the groups and the schedule of the federation's Schedule Proposal, men and women, with the classification ties of Monday.

Tap a game to enter its three matches: pick each region's pair and type the set scores (from the first region's side). A third set is the super tie-break: type its points, e.g. 10-6 — it counts as 7-6. Press Save after each match.

Competition setup

Day, time and court of a game can be changed when you open it. Start again deletes every result and recreates the Etapa I schedule.
