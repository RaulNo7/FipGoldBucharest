Teams come from the official entry lists (89 pairs). Eliminated teams are greyed out — manage them on the Teams tab. The match format is fixed for the whole tournament: best of 3 sets · star point (two advantages, then golden point) · tiebreak to 7, win by 2.

Overlay display

OBS overlay URL

In OBS: Sources → + → Browser → paste this URL. Set width 1920, height 1080, tick "transparent". Sizing the source as a tight crop instead? Use at least 700 × 330 at scale 1 — the scorebug sits 40px from the edge and the winner banner adds height, so anything smaller clips the score columns or title.

Court TV display

Full-screen score on a black background — open this on a TV / laptop / tablet on the same Wi-Fi. It keeps showing the final score even while the stream plays commercials.
