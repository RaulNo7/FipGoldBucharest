Match — select teams

Regional event: pick the competition, then for each side the region and its two players (or a pair already made on the Teams tab) and press Put on court. The scorebug shows the region's code and colour. Nobody is eliminated after a game.

Teams come from the official entry lists. Eliminated teams are greyed out — the pair that loses a match is eliminated automatically; manage exceptions on the Teams page.

Match format

Best of 3 sets · 6 games per set · tiebreak to 7, win by 2. The deuce rule takes effect at once (even mid-game); pick the 3rd-set format before the match starts — a deciding set already under way is not converted. Both are kept by "Reset score" and go back to the defaults (star point, normal set) with "Reset everything".
