Toggle a team to Eliminated after they lose their match. Eliminated teams stay visible here (greyed out) and cannot be picked for a match on the Score settings tab. Click a name (or ✎) to correct it — spelling, or a replacement player — then press Enter; ↺ restores the entry-list name. Changes apply everywhere instantly (pickers, scorebug, players intro) and survive a restart.

Player cards (padeltour.ro)

Reads each player's padeltour.ro profile (photo, age, county, side, points, PT level, record, titles, form) for the 🪪 player cards on the Media tab. Do it before the match — it takes a few seconds per pair and keeps working offline afterwards. ⟳ on a team reads just that pair. The chip next to a name shows when the profile was read; click it to set or change the player's profile link.
